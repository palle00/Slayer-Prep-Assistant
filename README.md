<h2>🛡️ Built around your account</h2>

<p>
SlayerPrepAssistant doesn't just show the most expensive setup from a guide.
</p>

<p>
It compares recommendations with what you currently have
<strong>equipped, in your inventory, and in your last-known bank</strong>.
</p>

<p>
With <strong>Best I Own</strong>, the plugin can pick the highest recommended item you already have instead of immediately pointing you toward an expensive upgrade.
</p>

<p>
You can also switch between different loadout modes depending on how you want to prepare.
</p>

<h2 align="center">🎒 Know exactly where your gear is</h2>

<p align="center">
  Equipment recommendations are matched against your current RuneLite state.
</p>

<br>

<div align="center">

| Status | Meaning |
|:---:|---|
| 🟢 **Equipped** | You're already wearing it |
| 🔵 **Inventory** | You're carrying it |
| 🟡 **Bank** | Found in your last-known bank |
| 🔴 **Missing** | Not found |
| ⚪ **Unknown** | Bank data isn't available yet |

</div>

<br>

> **SlayerPrepAssistant isn't trying to replace the Wiki.**  
> It's trying to stop you from opening it every five minutes.

<h2 align="center">📜 Wiki strategy, without the clutter</h2>

<p align="center">

<strong>Old School RuneScape Wiki</strong> and turns it into a compact RuneLite setup.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Choose target</strong>
  &nbsp;→&nbsp;
  <strong>Choose combat style</strong>
  &nbsp;→&nbsp;
  <strong>Choose loadout</strong>
  &nbsp;→&nbsp;
  <strong>Prepare</strong>
</p>

<p align="center">
If you want the full guide, you can still open the original Wiki page directly from the plugin.
</p>

---

## 🔒 Your information stays local

Your **bank, inventory, equipment, stats, username, and Slayer progress** are not sent to the OSRS Wiki.

External requests are only used to retrieve public strategy and item information.

SlayerPrepAssistant is also a preparation tool — it does **not** attack monsters, move your character, withdraw gear, equip items, or automate gameplay.
